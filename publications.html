Active use of latent tree-structured sentence representation in humans and large language models

Wei Liu, Ming Xiang, Nai Ding. Nature Human Behaviour 10(2), 303–316 (2026). Online 10 Sep 2025.

A novel inference task shows that both humans and LLMs construct and actively use tree-structured representations during comprehension, and presents an approach that reconstructs such representations from behavioral data.

Can Centaur truly simulate human cognition? The fundamental limitation of instruction understanding

Wei Liu, Nai Ding. National Science Open (perspective), 2026. Online 11 Dec 2025.

Identifies a fundamental limitation of instruction-following models in simulating human cognition, challenging the assumption that instruction-tuned LLMs are valid cognitive models.

Information Integration in Large Language Models is Gated by Linguistic Structural Markers

Wei Liu, Nai Ding. Proceedings of the 2025 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP 2025), 2025. Oral presentation

A windowed-prediction task characterizes the information-integration window of LLMs; the window is modulated by explicit syntactic cues such as punctuation.

MASP: A Multilingual Dataset for Probing Scalar Modifier Understanding in LLMs

Xinyu Gao, Nai Ding, Wei Liu (corresponding author). CCL 2025 · LNCS 16052, Springer (2026), pp. 281–300.

Open proceedings version: CCL 2025, pp. 1003–1019.

Extends scalar-modifier probing from English to Chinese, building a multilingual benchmark to evaluate how LLMs encode degree semantics across typologically different languages.

Jieyu Lin, Wei Liu, Jiajie Zou, Nai Ding. Proceedings of the 22nd Chinese National Conference on Computational Linguistics (CCL 2023), pp. 847–861, 2023.

Simple but Challenging: Natural Language Inference Models Fail on Simple Sentences

Cheng Luo, Wei Liu, Jieyu Lin, Jiajie Zou, Ming Xiang, Nai Ding. Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2022, pp. 3449–3462, 2022.

Constructs minimally-edited sentence pairs that flip NLI labels while preserving surface tokens, exposing systematic failures of strong NLI models on otherwise-simple inputs.
