Wei Liu

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the College of Biomedical Engineering and Instrument Science at Zhejiang University, working with Prof. Nai Ding.

My research investigates and compares language processing in humans and large language models. It draws on cognitive neuroscience, linguistics, and natural language processing to gain mechanistic insights into LLMs and the computational basis of human language processing.

Publications

Active use of latent tree-structured sentence representation in humans and large language models

Wei Liu, Ming Xiang, Nai Ding. Nature Human Behaviour 10(2), 303–316 (2026). Online 10 Sep 2025.

A novel inference task shows that both humans and LLMs construct and actively use tree-structured representations during comprehension; an approach reconstructs such representations from behavioral data.

Can Centaur truly simulate human cognition? The fundamental limitation of instruction understanding

Wei Liu, Nai Ding. National Science Open (perspective), 2026 (online 11 Dec 2025).

Identifies a fundamental limitation of instruction-following models in simulating human cognition, challenging the assumption that instruction-tuned LLMs are valid cognitive models.

Information Integration in Large Language Models is Gated by Linguistic Structural Markers

Wei Liu, Nai Ding. EMNLP 2025. Oral presentation

A windowed-prediction task characterizes LLMs' information-integration window, showing it is modulated by explicit syntactic cues such as punctuation.
